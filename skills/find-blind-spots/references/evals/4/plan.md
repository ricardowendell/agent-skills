# Plan: self-serve trial onboarding

## Goals

Cut the time from signup to a first successful run, without adding a sales step.
The trial should feel like the product, not like a demo of it.

## Work

- Replace the sales-assisted demo call with a guided in-product tour.
- Give every trial a sandbox workspace that survives the trial period.
- Add a three-step setup checklist to the dashboard.
- Send one reminder email at day three if the checklist is still incomplete.

## Risks

- The tour doubles support tickets if the sandbox is slow to provision.
- Users may skip the checklist and never come back to it.
- The reminder email is easy to mute, so it cannot be the only nudge.
