---
description: Sync a branch with its base safely: fetch, fast-forward or rebase/merge, stop on conflict
---

Use the `sync-branch` skill to bring the branch described below up to date with its base. Read the project policy first, capture the pre-state, fetch, handle a dirty tree, then fast-forward or rebase/merge as the policy says. Stop at the first conflict and name the continue/abort command. Never force-push, never `reset --hard`, and never resolve a conflict by discarding one side.
